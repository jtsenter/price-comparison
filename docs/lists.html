My Lists

Frequency is built in and fixed: every product is Weekly, Monthly, Rare or Archived, and only ever one of them. My lists are yours to make, and they overlap freely - a product can be in as many as you like. Each one also gets a filter button on the main screen; the eye turns that button off without touching the list itself, for the ones you file into but rarely filter by.

You're viewing the read-only demo, so lists can't be created or changed here. What you see is the owner's set-up.
